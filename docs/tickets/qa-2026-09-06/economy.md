## What happened / expected

Saved stars feel purposeless when current items are progress-locked or already owned.

Propose a small non-punitive loop: an immediately understandable first cosmetic choice, wardrobe purchases and optional village/beacon decoration goals, with repeatable earning objectives rather than mandatory grinding.

## Reproduction / evaluation scenario

Inspect balances of 0, 25 and 100 saved stars with early/late progress and with all current cosmetics owned.

- [ ] No pay-to-learn, loss of earned ownership, artificial taxes, cash purchases, login system or paid asset generation.
