## Important decisions and evidence

- Incorrect grading comes before increased difficulty. trim/nest are absent from
  accepted verb metadata; checkpoint generation can draw valid homographs as
  supposed distractors. Fix both, not just the two reported strings.
- #6 reopens the earlier pathing completion: prior first-direction checks did not
  prove walkable geometry. The module is roughly 15KB, not an enormous engine.
  A small authored route graph plus doorway/transition anchors is the preferred
  replacement. Camera/landing movement is a separate coordinate problem (#26).
- #10 is not a new duplicate. A concrete call-order lead already exists: initial
  story speech starts before opening an overlay that cancels narration. iPad
  autoplay remains a separate check. No fix is claimed in this ticket pass.
- Classic-only limits/difficulty and gentle/free Learning are the default proposal,
  preserving the teacher-led mode split. Say so before implementing changed rules.
- Two type reveals are explicit owner intent. The proposed THREE separate
  meaning-picture helps and replenishment costs are NOT approved numbers.
- Word stars are non-spendable practice evidence, not another wallet. Proposed
  2/6/12 XP thresholds and 1/3/6 practice-day gates need approval/calibration.
  Six days encountering a word is not six app visits; 496 words make that distinction
  important. Shared-device classroom play cannot diagnose an individual pupil's
  mastery. No names/accounts are introduced by this proposal.
- Saved cosmetic stars already have prices AND progress gates. #17 explains them
  now; #30 designs better choices without confiscating saves or adding grind taxes.
- Paid voice samples/clips and generated art require explicit budget approval.
  Code models orchestrate asset tools; choosing Astra does not itself generate
  better sprite pixels. Sprites remain sheet-based when generation is approved.
