# Windwords

A teacher-directed word-class adventure within Pip’s English.

## Language

**Pip**: The red-panda explorer and recurring Pip’s English mascot.
**Learning**: Guided practice with free explanations and gentle retries.
**Classic**: The optional branching adventure with limited reveals and run aids.
**Run**: One journey from Sky Islands until victory, defeat or return to village.
**Region**: A stage of the journey: Sky Islands, Floating Forest, Crystal Peaks or Ember Volcano.
**Route**: One of two paths through a region, sometimes with Classic modifiers.
**Beacon**: The multi-island checkpoint at the end of a region.
**Picture sense**: One word interpreted as a noun, verb, adjective or adverb.
**Reveal**: Showing and explaining a picture sense’s word class in Classic.
**Run stars**: Stars available for temporary supplies during the current Classic run.
**Saved stars**: Lifetime cosmetic currency retained between runs on this device.
**Supply stop**: An optional Classic checkpoint shopping visit; village access is preview/cosmetics.
**Run aid**: A temporary rescue, heart refill or extra reveal bought for one run.
**Wardrobe**: Planned place to preview outfits freely and keep only owned outfits when leaving.
